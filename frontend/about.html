About WashCare India

WashCare India connects customers with verified, experienced washing machine technicians for doorstep repair, installation, uninstallation and general service — currently across major cities, with more being added as our technician network grows.

What we do

We handle the booking, coordination and follow-up so you don't have to search for a technician yourself. Tell us your city, the problem and your preferred time, and our team takes it from there — confirming the visit and assigning a technician near you.

Our approach

We believe in transparent communication before any work begins, and we're upfront about what we can and can't promise: our technician network is growing city by city, and coverage and response times vary by location.
